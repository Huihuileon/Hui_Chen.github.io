I am Hui Chen, a student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). I am interested in theoretical condensed matter physics and the collective behavior of quantum many-body systems.

My interests include quantum phases and critical phenomena, boundary and low-dimensional physics, and nonequilibrium dynamics and transport.

I welcome discussions about condensed matter theory and reading in many-body physics. Please feel free to contact me at chenhui262@mails.ucas.ac.cn.
